Chromalith — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23 · Version 1.0.0

Overview

Chromalith is a puzzle game. We designed it to collect as little data as possible. This page explains what the game stores on your device and, in the App Store version, what our advertising and purchase partners process.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (current level, stars, gem balance, settings and language) is saved locally on your device. It is never uploaded to a server by the game itself. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Advertising (App Store version)

The App Store version shows ads served through third-party advertising networks (for example AdMob). These partners may use device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, subject to your consent choices and Apple's App Tracking Transparency rules. We do not sell personal data ourselves. The web version you may play in a browser contains no ad SDKs and no tracking.

Purchases

In-app purchases (removing ads, gem packs) are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

The game contains no violence, no chat and no user-generated content. It is suitable for all ages.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, open a GitHub issue at the project repository or contact the developer listed on the App Store page.
